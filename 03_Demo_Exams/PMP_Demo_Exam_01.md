# PMP® Demo Exam 01

### Based on the *PMBOK® Guide – Eighth Edition* and the PMP® Examination Content Outline (July 2026)

| Item | Real exam | This demo exam |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | 180 (170 scored + 10 unscored pretest) | 180 (all scored) |
| Time | 240 minutes | 240 minutes |
| Breaks | Two optional 10-minute breaks | After Q60 and after Q120 |
| Domains | People 33% · Process 41% · Business Environment 26% | Same weighting |
| Approaches | ~40% predictive, ~60% agile/hybrid | Same mix |

**How to take this exam:** set a timer for 240 minutes, sit somewhere quiet, and answer every question without notes. Write your answers on paper (question number + letter). There is no penalty for guessing, so never leave a question blank. Pace yourself at about **80 seconds per question** — you should be near Q60 at 80 minutes and Q120 at 160 minutes. Only after finishing, check the [Answer Key](#answer-key) and read the [explanations](#explanations), then complete the [score analysis](#score-analysis).

*Question types:* most questions have one best answer. Multiple-response questions say how many options to choose. Matching questions ask you to pair items with options. Fill-in-the-blank questions need a number. Case-study sets share one scenario across several questions.

*On screen:* the same exam runs in the PMP Exam Simulator (`04_PMP_Exam_Simulator.html`), which times it, scores it by domain and task, and then gives you a PDF with the correct answer and the explanation for every question you missed.

> #### Case Study — Crestline Bank open-banking platform
>
> Crestline Bank is building an open-banking API platform with three Scrum teams that coordinate through a weekly scrum of scrums. The platform must go live in nine months.
>
