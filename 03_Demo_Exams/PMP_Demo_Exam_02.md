# PMP® Demo Exam 02

### Based on the *PMBOK® Guide – Eighth Edition* and the PMP® Examination Content Outline (July 2026)

| Item | Real exam | This demo exam |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | 180 (170 scored + 10 unscored pretest) | 180 (all scored) |
| Time | 240 minutes | 240 minutes |
| Breaks | Two optional 10-minute breaks | After Q60 and after Q120 |
| Domains | People 33% · Process 41% · Business Environment 26% | Same weighting |
| Approaches | ~40% predictive, ~60% agile/hybrid | Same mix |

**How to take this exam:** set a timer for 240 minutes, sit somewhere quiet, and answer every question without notes. Write your answers on paper (question number + letter). There is no penalty for guessing, so never leave a question blank. Pace yourself at about **80 seconds per question** — you should be near Q60 at 80 minutes and Q120 at 160 minutes. Only after finishing, check the [Answer Key](#answer-key) and read the [explanations](#explanations), then complete the [score analysis](#score-analysis).

*Question types:* most questions have one best answer. Multiple-response questions say how many options to choose. Matching questions ask you to pair items with options. Fill-in-the-blank questions need a number. Case-study sets share one scenario across several questions.

*On screen:* the same exam runs in the PMP Exam Simulator (`04_PMP_Exam_Simulator.html`), which times it, scores it by domain and task, and then gives you a PDF with the correct answer and the explanation for every question you missed.

## Detailed Explanations

**Q1 — Answer: A** · *People · PE1 Develop a common vision · Predictive*

*Correct:* A. Objectives, success criteria, roles, and how decisions will be made

Kickoffs align everyone on why, what success looks like, who does what and how decisions are made. Technical detail, threats or personal preferences do not build shared direction.

**Q2 — Answer: B** · *People · PE2 Manage conflicts · Predictive*

*Correct:* B. Withdraw temporarily to allow a short cooling-off period first

When emotions are high, a short, temporary withdrawal can allow people to calm down. The issue must then be addressed — withdrawal is never a permanent solution.

**Q3 — Answer: D** · *Process · PR4 Plan and manage resources · Predictive*

*Correct:* D. Present options with trade-offs, such as more resources or re-prioritised work

Resource constraints can extend schedules; the PM gives decision-makers options and trade-offs rather than ignoring the constraint.

**Q4 — Answer: D** · *People · PE1 Develop a common vision · Predictive*

*Correct:* D. Facilitate a workshop to agree shared objectives and success measures

A common vision requires agreed objectives and success measures. Choosing one group's view or reporting against conflicting definitions leaves stakeholders misaligned and invites conflict later.

**Q5 — Answer: A** · *Process · PR4 Plan and manage resources · Predictive*

*Correct:* A. Resource breakdown structure

The RBS organises resources by category (labour, equipment, materials) and type, supporting estimating and control.

**Q6 — Answer: A** · *People · PE8 Plan and manage communication · Agile*

*Correct:* A. Short decision records stored in the team's shared repository

Lightweight decision records in a shared repository (pull communication) preserve rationale efficiently. Email minutes get lost, and verbal explanations depend on one person.

**Q7 — Answer: B** · *Process · PR2 Develop and manage project scope · Hybrid*

*Correct:* B. An excitement (delighter) feature

Unexpected features that create delight are excitement features. Over time they tend to become expected performance or basic features.

**Q8 — Answer: D** · *Business Environment · BE6 Continuous improvement · Agile*

*Correct:* D. Support the experiment and agree how success will be judged

Continuous improvement works through small, measurable experiments that the team owns and reviews.

**Q9 — Answer: B** · *People · PE5 Align stakeholder expectations · Predictive*

*Correct:* B. Clarify the prototype's purpose and agree suitable acceptance criteria

Aligning expectations means agreeing what each deliverable is for and how it will be judged. Treating a usability prototype like a final product wastes time and money.

**Q10 — Answer: C** · *People · PE4 Engage stakeholders · Predictive*

*Correct:* C. Engage them locally, in their language and through respected intermediaries

Culturally appropriate, accessible engagement is essential for stakeholders whose support determines success. Distant meetings and top-down mandates ignore their context.

**Q11 — Answer: B** · *Process · PR10 Manage project closure · Hybrid*

*Correct:* B. Benefit tracking is formally handed over to the business benefit owner

Benefit ownership transfers to the business at closure so that measurement continues after the project ends.

**Q12 — Answer: C** · *Process · PR6 Plan and manage finance · Predictive*

*Correct:* C. The approved time-phased budget, including contingency but not management reserves

The cost baseline includes contingency reserves for identified risks; management reserves are added on top to form the project budget.

**Q13 — Answer: A, D** · *Business Environment · BE4 Remove impediments and manage issues · Agile*

*Correct:* A. The team lacks access to a test environment / D. A month-long approval process for buying a test tool the team needs

Impediments are obstacles to the team's progress, especially organisational ones. Selecting and ordering work, and writing code, are the responsibilities of the developers and the product owner.

**Q14 — Answer: B** · *Process · PR6 Plan and manage finance · Predictive*

*Correct:* B. Project B, because it has the higher NPV

When projects are mutually exclusive, the one with the higher NPV adds the most value in absolute terms.

**Q15 — Answer: D** · *Process · PR8 Plan and manage schedule · Hybrid*

*Correct:* D. As an external dependency to be tracked and coordinated with the city

Dependencies on parties outside the project are external and usually beyond the team's control, so they need close tracking and coordination.

**Q16 — Answer: D** · *People · PE1 Develop a common vision · Agile*

*Correct:* D. Refine it to state the target users, their need and the key differentiating benefit

A useful vision identifies who the product is for, what need it meets and why it is different (for example, using the elevator-pitch format). A feature list is a backlog, not a vision.

**Q17 — Answer: A** · *People · PE5 Align stakeholder expectations · Hybrid*

*Correct:* A. Engage the regulator early to agree the evidence needed and realistic review milestones
