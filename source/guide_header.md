# PMBOK® Guide – Eighth Edition & PMP® Exam — Complete Study Guide

**A self-study companion for the *PMBOK® Guide – Eighth Edition* (PMI, 2025) and the PMP® Examination Content Outline that took effect in July 2026**

> **Read this first.** This is an independent study aid written to help you understand, remember and *apply* the content you will be tested on. It explains the concepts of the *PMBOK® Guide – Eighth Edition* in plain language and connects them to the way PMP® questions are written. It does not reproduce the PMBOK® Guide, so keep your copy of the Guide next to you and use this book to make sense of it. Exam logistics (number of questions, time, fees, eligibility) can change, so confirm them in PMI's current *PMP Certification Handbook* before you book your exam. ## How to study with this guide

1. **Read actively.** Each chapter starts with what you should be able to do afterwards. Keep a notebook of terms you could not explain to a colleague.
2. **Test yourself honestly.** Answer the review questions without looking back. Mark any question you guessed as "wrong" even if you guessed right.
3. **Close the gaps.** If you score below 80% on a chapter, re-read the parts you missed before moving on.
4. **Integrate.** After the last chapter, take the Comprehensive Question Bank. It mixes topics the way the exam does.
5. **Simulate.** Take the demo exams under exam conditions: 240 minutes, no notes, breaks only after questions 60 and 120. Review *every* explanation — including those for questions you got right.
6. **Think like PMI.** Chapter 21 explains the mindset behind the "best" answer. Re-read it before every demo exam.

### Conventions used in this guide

- **Exam tip** boxes tell you how a concept tends to appear in questions.
- **Trap** boxes describe a wrong answer that looks attractive.
- **What changed** boxes compare the Eighth Edition with the Sixth and Seventh Editions — useful if you have older materials.
- Process names follow the Eighth Edition; when an older name is still common in prep materials it is given in brackets, e.g. *Develop Scope Structure (formerly Create WBS)*.
