# PMBOK® Guide – Eighth Edition & PMP® — Comprehensive Question Bank (with Answers)

**Purpose.** This question bank checks your knowledge of the *whole* body of material in the Study Guide: the Standard (value delivery, environment, principles), the seven performance domains and their 40 processes, quality, agile and hybrid delivery, people skills, the business environment, tailoring, the toolbox of models/methods/artifacts, and calculations. The final parts mix topics in realistic scenarios, the way the exam does.

**How to use it**

1. Work through one part at a time without notes. Write down your answers.
2. Check the answers at the end of the part. Each answer has a short explanation — read it even when you were right.
3. Record your score per part in the table below. Anything under **80%** shows a topic to revisit in the Study Guide (the chapter references are given in the table).
4. Come back to the parts you scored lowest on a week later.

**Question types.** Most questions have one best answer. Multiple-response questions tell you how many options to choose; matching questions ask you to pair items; fill-in-the-blank questions need a number. As on the real exam, a multiple-response or matching question counts as correct only if every part is right.
