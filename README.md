# PMBOK® Guide 8 & PMP® Exam Prep Package

An independent self-study package for the **PMP® exam**, built around the *PMBOK® Guide – Eighth Edition* and the PMP Examination Content Outline that took effect in July 2026.

At the end of every attempt it gives you a **PDF of your corrections**: each question you missed, with the correct answer, your answer, the explanation and the Study Guide chapter to revise. It works offline in any modern browser. | 1 file |

The demo exams contain 1,800 questions. Most have a single best answer; the rest are multiple-response ("Choose two"), matching and numeric fill-in questions, and every exam includes case-study sets.

## How to study (8 weeks, about 10–12 hours a week)

1. **Weeks 1–6: read the Study Guide.** Read one chapter at a time and answer its review questions *before* you check Appendix B. If you score below 80%, re-read the parts you missed. The full week-by-week plan is at the start of the guide.
2. **Weeks 6–7: work through the Question Bank**, one part at a time and without notes. Record your score for each part and go back to the chapters listed for any part below 80%.
3. **Weeks 7–8 and beyond: take the demo exams on the platform**, one per sitting, as a timed exam. When you finish, download the corrections PDF and work through it: read each explanation, then revise the Study Guide chapter it points to. Use quick quizzes on "Answered wrongly last time" to check that the gaps are closed. If you consistently score 75% or more, you are in a strong position.
4. Re-read Chapter 21 ("Exam Strategy and the PMI Mindset") before every demo exam.

## The exam platform

Open `04_PMP_Exam_Simulator.html` by double-clicking it. It needs no installation or internet connection; without one it uses your system fonts.

- **Timed exam:** 180 questions in 240 minutes, in three sections of 60, with an optional 10-minute break after each of the first two sections. Once you end a section it is locked. You can flag questions, cross out options and use the on-screen calculator. The exam is submitted automatically when time runs out.
- **Ten demo exams plus mixed exams:** each mixed exam is a new 180-question exam drawn from all ten demo exams. It keeps the real domain weighting and about 40% predictive questions, includes two complete case studies, and uses questions you have not answered yet first.
- **Corrections PDF:** when an attempt ends, the results screen has a **Download PDF** button. The PDF starts with your score by domain and delivery approach and the tasks to focus on. It then lists every question you got wrong or left unanswered, showing the correct answer, your answer, the explanation and the Study Guide chapter to revise. Case-study scenarios are printed with their questions.
- **Practice:** untimed, and shows the correct answer and its explanation after each question.
- **Quick quiz:** 10–60 random questions from all ten exams. You can filter by domain or delivery approach, or limit the quiz to questions you have not answered yet or got wrong last time.
- **Results:** your score with an indicative rating for each domain, delivery approach and task (the 26 tasks of the Exam Content Outline), plus a full answer review on screen.
- **Keyboard:** <kbd>A</kbd>–<kbd>E</kbd> or <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>5</kbd> choose an option, <kbd>F</kbd> flags the question, <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> move between questions, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> checks the answer or moves on.
- **Saving:** progress and results are stored in the browser you use, so opening the file in a different browser starts fresh.
- **Online copy:** the same platform is published at <https://claude.ai/artifact/W6r3Pg7qx7KQKP6tpaoBDF>. It is private to your Claude account, and results you finish there are saved to your account so they appear on every device where you open it. The corrections PDF works there too: your browser asks you to confirm the download.

## Exam facts used — please verify

These materials follow the PMP exam as described in the 2026 Examination Content Outline:

- 180 questions (170 scored and 10 unscored pretest questions)
- 240 minutes, with two optional 10-minute breaks
- Domains: People 33%, Process 41%, Business Environment 26%
- About 40% of questions on predictive approaches and 60% on agile or hybrid approaches

Exam logistics change from time to time. Confirm them in PMI's current *PMP Certification Handbook* before you book your exam.
